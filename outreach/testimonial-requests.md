# Testimonial gathering — sourcing list + request templates

Goal: collect text testimonials to seed the YouTube agency profile (and back-fill
the sales profile). Upwork off-platform testimonials can be added to portfolio items
and quoted in the overview. Aim for 3 short ones to start.

---

## Where to find people to ask (you said none came to mind — start here)
1. **Infivision Media** — whoever you reported to. The 100%-growth proof. Ask first.
2. **Anyone on the content team you built/managed** — a peer/team testimonial still
   reads as real proof that you led a YouTube operation.
3. **Past managers/founders** from any role where you touched a channel — scan your
   LinkedIn connections, even informal advice counts if the outcome was real.
4. **The 1L client-ladder client** (tasks/context.md) — once they post the jobs,
   each closed milestone is a fresh Upwork review, not just a text quote.
5. **Sales clients** (Squadstack, SidekeyTV) — for the SALES profile only, kept
   separate from YouTube per the hard-separation rule.
6. **Friends/founders you advised on their channel** — even unpaid, if you can name a
   concrete before/after.

Rule: never invent a quote. If someone can only confirm "Flavio managed our channel
and was reliable," that's still usable — don't dress it up with numbers they didn't say.

---

## Template A — YouTube client / manager (warm)
```
Hey [Name], hope you're well.

I'm setting up a YouTube agency profile and pulling together a few short
testimonials from people I've worked with. Could you spare 2 minutes for a couple
of lines on the channel work we did together at [Infivision / company]?

If it's easier, I can send you a draft to edit so it takes 30 seconds. Thanks [Name].

Flavio
```

## Template B — make-it-easy (pre-drafted, they just approve/edit)
```
Hey [Name], quick one.

Gathering testimonials for a new profile. To save you time I drafted something you
can edit or approve as-is:

"Flavio managed our YouTube channel for [duration] and grew it
[outcome, e.g. doubling views and subs over six months]. He built the systems and
the team behind it, not just the uploads. Reliable, and he actually understood what
moved the numbers."

Happy to change anything that doesn't sound like you. Thanks [Name].

Flavio
```

## Template C — team member / peer
```
Hey [Name], hope things are good.

I'm building out a profile and would love a short line from someone who worked on
the [Infivision / company] content team with me. Just a couple of sentences on what it
was like working together and what we shipped. I can draft it if that's easier.

Thanks a lot.

Flavio
```
