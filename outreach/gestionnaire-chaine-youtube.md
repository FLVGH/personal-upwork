## Bid + boost

Do not bid $100 for the job they described. Bid $100 for the first thing that job actually
needs, and say what the rest costs later. If you quote $100 against the full scope you have
set your own price for daily channel management at $100 and you will never move it with this
client.

**Boost: no.** Not at this budget. Spending boost connects to win $100 is a loss on the
connects alone.
