Your med school video went up yesterday and it is sitting at 266 views. I will start there, since it is the part you asked about twice, and answer the rest underneath.

The title is the problem. HOW TO GET INTO MED SCHOOL is a phrase owned by channels a hundred times your size, and there is nothing in it only Dr. Malonga could have said. Three things I would do:

2. Split the video. There are thirteen steps for traditional applicants and then nine more for non-traditional ones. Those are two different viewers. The college junior leaves at the pivot, and the 32 year old career changer sat through fifteen minutes that were not for them to reach their half. Two videos, both landing fully, and the non-traditional one has a fraction of the competition.

The handle is thedoctorinvestor and the video is admissions advice, so the recommendation system is working around the channel rather than with it. And a twenty five minute video built on thirteen numbered steps has no chapters, which means none of those steps can surface on their own.

On my approach generally, that is it in miniature. Packaging before keywords. Keyword research tells you what people search. It does not tell you why they click one result and not the other, and on a channel this size the click is the whole bottleneck.

Channels I have grown: Infivision Media, a year and a half, where I ran the content team and doubled growth six consecutive months. Writers, editors, a designer, and I owned the packaging call on every upload. Six years on channels before and alongside that, all of them other people's.

Straight answer on your niche question: no physician creators. What I have worked on is the same shape though, credentialed people explaining their own field, and the failure is always identical. They write titles for peers instead of for the person who does not know the field yet. That video is doing exactly that.

I can commit 30 hours a week.

Up for a call tomorrow?
