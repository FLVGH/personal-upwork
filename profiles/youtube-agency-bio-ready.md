## Honest strategic flag (read before you commit connects)
Your **personal** profile is Top Rated, 100% JSS, 17 jobs, $10K earned. A brand-new
agency starts at **zero** of all that — it cannot inherit your track record. For most
small YouTube jobs Upwork's algorithm favors the established individual.

Recommended hybrid (no regret):
- Set the agency profile up now — it's free and gives you a home for the YouTube brand.
- Keep bidding YouTube jobs from your **personal Top Rated profile** until the agency
  has its own reviews and you have a vetted editor on hand.
- Flip new YouTube bids to "on behalf of agency" once the agency has 1-2 reviews.

This lets you build the agency without throwing away the trust you already earned.
