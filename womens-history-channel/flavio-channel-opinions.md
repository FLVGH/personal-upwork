## Too much AI — the look to avoid

- Grim History
- History Before Sleep
- Sleepy History with Oliver
- Storytime English
- **What They Buried West**
- Civilizations Explained

**Flag this one directly: What They Buried West is tagged here as "too much AI," but it's the
single strongest growth data point in the entire roadmap** — 11,200 subs off only 61 videos,
+919,313 views in 30 days, the highest views-per-subscriber ratio of anything on the list, and
it's the channel I pointed to earlier as the closest direct comp. Flavio's visual judgment and
the growth numbers are pointing in opposite directions on this one specific channel. That's not
something to resolve quietly — it's a real question for all three of you: chase the growth
pattern even if the visual execution isn't what you want to be known for, or treat the numbers
as "AI channels can still win right now" without adopting that exact look. Worth its own five
minutes on the Day 1 call.
