Open this on your phone when you sit down. Two versions: the first one, and every one after.

## SIT 1 — profile then two bids (do this one first, once)

You cannot do five bids in the first hour because the profile is not live yet. Trying to is
how you burn 80 connects pointing at a bio that says the wrong thing.

| Min | Do | From |

Entry 3 is written now. The finance channel numbers you gave me for the Wichita proposal
were exactly what that slot was waiting for, so all three are ready to paste.

**20-25 min, the routine.** Copy the prompt block out of `tasks/job-hunt-routine.md` into
claude.ai/code/routines, replacing what is stored there. The repo is fixed, the scheduler is
not, and until you do this it keeps running the old editing and thumbnail queries.

**25-60 min, two bids.** Job #6 (Wichita, founder-led) is already send-ready in
`outreach/youtube-growth-strategist-founder-brand.md`. That one is a paste. Then pick one more
off the inbox and say "draft 1" in the phone chat.

Two bids, not five. The first hour buys you a profile that does not undercut everything after it.

## SIT 2 ONWARDS — five bids in an hour

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 0-5 | Read `tasks/job-inbox.md`. Pick 5 off BID and BORDERLINE |
| 5-12 | Open all 5. Kill any that closed. Check applicant count and the Insights avg rate |
| 12-52 | "draft 2", "draft 5"... one at a time, 8 min each |
| 52-60 | Paste into Upwork, submit, tell me which ones went |

**The 5-12 minute check is the one people skip.** The inbox is a snapshot from whenever the
routine ran. A job at 3 applicants when it was scraped is often at 20 by the time you open it,
and the Insights avg hourly is the number your bid keys off. Both change. Neither is in the
inbox.

If a job is gone or crowded, drop it and move on. Do not talk yourself into it.

**Do not draft five and then send five.** Send each one as it is finished. Sitting on finished
proposals is how an hour turns into zero bids.

## How to make small changes

You do not need a session for this. Say it in the phone chat and it becomes a rule in the repo.

- "stop marking $500 fixed as borderline, that's a retainer" → scoring rule in `job-hunt-routine.md`
- "the opener sounds like a cover letter" → I rewrite and the fix goes into CLAUDE.md sec. 16
- "add appointment setter back to the queries" → query list
- "I can't defend the 250K number anymore" → `tasks/context.md`, and no proposal uses it again

The pattern is the same every time: say what is wrong in plain words, I change the file, the
next run picks it up. That is the whole maintenance model. Nothing needs a big rebuild.

The one thing that does NOT work this way: the routine prompt stored at claude.ai/code/routines.
That one lives outside the repo and only you can edit it. Everything else, just say it.
