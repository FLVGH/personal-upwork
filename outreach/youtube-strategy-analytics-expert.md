Six years on YouTube, all of it on channels I did not own. A year and a half running the content team at Infivision Media, growth doubled six months running, and the thing that moved it was packaging rather than upload volume.

What I would do first on yours is separate impressions from click-through, because they fail for opposite reasons and the fix for one makes the other worse.
