# 2-Week Launch Checklist — Women's History Channel

Goal: 5 videos fully produced (scripted, voiced, edited, thumbnailed) and buffered before the
channel is called "started." Built 2 Sep 2026 from the 23 Aug roadmap plus what Flavio described
about the 3-person split. Dated relative to whenever you actually kick this off — call that Day 1.

---

## Day 1 — decisions, not content (all 3 partners, one sitting, ~30-45 min)

Nothing gets made until these are settled, because they change what gets made:

- [ ] Channel name and handle
- [ ] Read and mark up `partner-agreement-draft.md` — roles, stop-loss, and especially the
      revenue/equity split, which nothing in the roadmap or chat ever specified
- [ ] Decide: pure Template A for videos 1-5, or blend A/B from video 1 (recommendation in the
      partner doc is pure A first)
- [ ] Decide: test Template C at all, or skip it entirely (roadmap: zero evidence either way)
- [ ] Confirm the ₹2 lakh / Video 10-20-40 / 20K-override stop-loss in writing

Same day, in parallel:
- [ ] Joy starts sourcing an editor and thumbnail person, and pulls test quotes/samples
- [ ] Joy tests vidIQ's built-in voiceover against ElevenLabs on any short sample text, so a
      choice exists before video 1's script is ready
- [ ] Flavio produces Video Brief #1 (Alice Ball is already named, so this is the easiest first
      rep — see `video-brief-template.md`)

## Days 2-10 — the daily engine

**Flavio, every day, 1-2 hours:** one video brief, using the template, moving down
`video-ideas-log.md`. Don't skip ahead to a title you like more without finishing research on
the current one — a half-researched brief just becomes Rishabh's problem.

**Rishabh:** turn each brief into a script within 24-48 hours of receiving it. If the queue ever
gets more than 2 briefs ahead of scripts, that's the signal to slow briefs down for a day rather
than let a backlog build silently.

**Joy, once the editor is hired (target: by day 3-4):** first job is a cheap 50-second teaser cut
of whatever script is ready first, testing the voice tool and the editing style (high-contrast
figure + one strategic element — map, ship, weapon — beats a plain portrait per the roadmap).
Greenlight the full 12-15 minute cut only once that teaser looks right, not before.

Rough pace if this holds: by end of day 10 you should have 5 briefs done, 4-5 scripts done, and
2-3 videos through a first edit pass.

## Days 11-14 — finish the buffer, don't publish yet

- [ ] All 5 of the first videos: scripted, voiced, edited, thumbnailed
- [ ] Watch all 5 back to back as a team before anything goes live — this is the cheapest point
      to catch a tone or pacing problem, before it's public
- [ ] Titles locked (Template A/B as decided on Day 1), thumbnails follow the high-contrast rule
- [ ] Upload schedule set for the first 5 (roadmap implies faceless + AI voice supports a
      steady cadence, not a single dump — spread them rather than posting all 5 in one day)

## What "started" means here

Not the first upload. It's: 5 videos sitting finished and buffered, a name and handle live, the
partner doc signed by all three, and the stop-loss gates written down somewhere all three of you
will actually look at again at Video 10.

## After launch — the stop-loss gates, so they don't get forgotten

- **Video 10:** first real read. Anchor to trailing 30-day views per video, not lifetime channel
  averages elsewhere. 5,000+ views per video (the roadmap's floor) on the trailing average is on
  track.
- **Video 20:** second read, same anchor.
- **Video 40 / ₹2 lakh:** the hard stop-loss point if nothing has broken 20K views by then. A
  single video clearing 20K at any point overrides the clock on whichever gate you're sitting at.

## Open items this checklist did not resolve (need the team, not more research)

Everything in `partner-agreement-draft.md`'s open-decisions list, plus: who has publish authority
day to day once the process is running — waiting for all 3 partners to sign off on every single
video will bottleneck a daily-brief pace within a week.
