## Bid + boost

**Bid: $18.50/hr.**

Sec. 10 says bid $1 to $2 under the client's actual avg paid. Avg paid is $15.00, so the rule
points at $13.50. That is under your floor, so it does not apply cleanly here. Bidding $13.50
for a role where he explicitly wants a strategy owner and not a task-doer prices you as the
second thing. Lesson #1 in sec. 12 is this trap in reverse.

$18.50 is your floor, not a round number, and only $3.50 over his historical avg. Defensible
because his spend history is upload and task work, and this is not that.
