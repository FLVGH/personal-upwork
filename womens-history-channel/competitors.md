# Direct Competitors

Channels doing the *same idea* as this one, not just the same broad niche. Different list from
`roadmap.md`'s 27 — those are channels to learn technique from. This list is who else is
already fishing the exact same water. Track these once the channel exists via vidIQ's own
competitor-follow feature (needs an authorized owned channel first); until then, tracked here.

---

## The Unnamed Life (@TheUnnamedLife-POV)

Found 11 Sep 2026 while checking a specific video link.

- **116 subscribers, 31 videos.** Posting regularly, hasn't broken out yet.
- **Channel description, verbatim:** "This channel explores dark female POV stories from
  history, espionage, war, and hidden power. From palace prisoners and tragic royal women, and
  girls surviving brutal regimes, every story puts you inside a life shaped by danger, secrecy,
  and control."
- **This is the same idea as this project**, not adjacent to it: dark-framed, female-focused,
  historical injustice stories. The difference is format, not concept — second-person POV
  narration ("You are 6 years old when the king's men come for you...") instead of third-person
  biography.
- **Confirmed fully AI-image based** — their own video description states it outright
  ("Images generated via AI tools for storytelling purposes") plus a dramatization disclaimer.
  Real data point for the content-type question, not inferred.

### The video that surfaced this channel

"Your Tragic Life as The 'Monster' of The Palace | Eugenia Martínez" — posted 21 Aug 2026.
1,351 views, 6 likes, 11:50 runtime.

**This is the exact same subject as Forgotten Lives' single biggest video ever** (3.43M views,
see `roadmap.md`/the Comp Channel Ledger artifact). The Unnamed Life took a proven top performer
from a direct comp and remade it with one twist: second-person POV instead of third-person.

**What that proves and doesn't:**
- Proves someone is already testing "clone the comp's best video with a stylistic twist" as a
  strategy. Real, live validation this approach exists, whether or not it's working yet.
- Does not prove the POV twist works — 1,351 views against the original's 3.43M is a steep
  drop, even accounting for channel age (116 subs is small). Could be discovery lag on a young
  channel, could be the twist genuinely not landing. One data point, not enough to call it
  either way.

### What this means for us

- **Direct validation the concept has demand** — someone else independently arrived at the same
  "dark female history POV" idea and is actively producing it, which is a signal the space
  isn't imagined, not a reason to worry about being first.
- **Their format choice (POV) is untested territory**, same as the roadmap's Template C —
  worth watching whether it ever breaks out, since it would answer a question our own Template
  A/B research couldn't.
- **Worth re-checking this channel monthly.** If POV starts working for them, that's evidence
  worth acting on. If they stay flat past 50-100 videos, that's evidence too.

## How to add another competitor here

Same shape as above: channel handle, subscriber/video count, their stated angle, and the
specific thing (a video, a title pattern, a format choice) that made it worth tracking. Not
every channel doing women's history belongs on this list — only ones close enough to this
channel's actual concept that a win or failure of theirs tells us something about ours.
