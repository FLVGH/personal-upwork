## Decisions made this session

**Firecrawl is out, permanently.** Tested live against the same search. Upwork silently
drops `payment_verified` and `proposals` from the URL on redirect, and the search page
carries no applicant count at all. It returned 10 jobs with no way to tell if any were
worth a connect. Apify returned `totalApplicants` on 25 of 25. Do not re-test this.
Firecrawl stays useful for reading one client site or channel page before a call.

**Score, do not filter.** The routine keeps every job and tags it BID / BORDERLINE / NO
with a reason. Flavio's call after the first door-check dropped 23 of 25 jobs invisibly.
He wants to review the rejects and catch bad scoring himself. Do not reintroduce silent
dropping.

**Volume starts small.** `maxResults` 5 per query across 4 YouTube queries, 20 jobs a
day. Raise it only when BID comes back empty two or three days running.

**Cost.** Apify is roughly $0.02/day at 20 jobs, likely inside the free credit. The
routine consumes normal Claude usage, one run a day. Connects remain the only real
expense and this changes nothing about them. The automation buys time, not cheaper bids.

**Model.** Opus 5 for now. Sonnet 5 would likely score 20 jobs just as well for a lot
less usage. Worth switching once the output has been sanity-checked a few times.
