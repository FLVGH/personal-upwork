## The Painful Historian (@ThePainfulHistorian) — new pattern, worth stealing

49.9K subs, 133 videos. **Not single biographies — this channel runs a "How Every X Died"
ranking/compilation format**, and it's their biggest lever by far:

| Title | Views |
|---|---|
| How Every Greek Hero Died | 937K |
| How Every Major Viking Leader Died | 637K |
| 1+ Hour Of The Worst Deaths In Medieval History | 440K |
| The Most EVIL Human Experiments That Actually Happened | 400K |
| Every Evil Emperor That Nearly Collapsed Rome | 317K |
| 1H+ Of The Worst Royal Deaths in History | 307K |

**This is a genuinely new, proven pattern our roadmap missed** — a ranking/compilation framed
around cause of death rather than the "brutal truths erased" listicle (Template B) or single
biography (Template A). It's not women-specific on this channel, but the shape transfers
directly: "How Every [Queen/Empress/Woman Executed as a Witch] Died" is an obvious gender-flip
worth testing as its own Template.

## The Story Bee (@thestorybee) — flag: this channel has left the history niche entirely

181K subs, 326 videos. Their historical videos (Elizabeth I — 626K views, Princess Diana — 638K,
a 1500s "ghost queen" — 1M) are all 4 years old. **Every recent upload is celebrity gossip** —
Kardashians, Selena Gomez, Shakira, Taylor Swift's wedding NDA, Britney Spears. Nothing history-
related has been posted recently. Flavio's own opinion file called this one "good content, CTR,
but not niche" — this confirms it more specifically: the channel didn't just start off-niche, it
pivoted away from history completely. Study their old historical titles and thumbnail/CTR
approach, but don't track it as an active comp going forward.

## What this changes

1. **A third proven pattern exists beyond Template A/B: the "How Every X Died" ranking format**
   (The Painful Historian). Worth testing as its own approach, gender-flipped.
2. **Confinement/imprisonment stories are a real, repeating subgenre** across three separate
   channels (Old Line Power, Forgotten Lives), not a one-off.
3. **Collaboration videos can dramatically outperform solo uploads** — 387K vs. 121K on the same
   channel. Worth planning for once there's something to trade.
4. **The Story Bee should come off the active-comp list** — it's not doing this content anymore.
5. **Four handles need correcting** before more research happens on those specific channels.
