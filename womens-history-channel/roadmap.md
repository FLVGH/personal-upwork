## Two proven title templates, plus one untested

**Template A — Biography.** `[Dark adjective] Life/Tale of [descriptor] | [Name]`. Proven at
scale for years (Forgotten Lives, female_tales, Dynastic Profiles). High ceiling — Forgotten
Lives' best single video: 3.43M views.

**Template B — Listicle erasure.** `[Number] Brutal/Disgusting Truths About [group] That
Hollywood/History Erased`. Proven explosive from zero in 2026 (What They Buried West, Sleepy
History with Oliver, Storytime English all launched this year and are breaking out with it).

**Template C — untested, no evidence either way.** Famous-name recognition (Cleopatra-style) and
"if not for her, we wouldn't have Y" counterfactual framing. Neither shows up as a working
pattern anywhere in the data. Test only as a Video 10 diagnostic candidate, not a safe bet.

## Stop-loss diagnostics

The Video 10 / Video 20 / Video 40 / 20K-override structure holds up, with two calibration notes:

- **Anchor to trailing 30-day averages, not lifetime ones.** Forgotten Lives' new videos pull
  ~30K now, not its 132K lifetime average. Still 6x the 5,000 success floor — don't expect
  100K-view videos on day one.
- **Expect a skewed distribution.** Most individual videos on working channels land under a few
  thousand views while a handful of breakouts carry the channel. "8 flops, 2 hits" in the first
  10 is the model working, not failing.

Keep the ₹2 lakh / Video 40 / 20K-override structure exactly as written.

## Production workflow, ₹3,000/video, 8/month, faceless

₹24,000/month total production spend. Rough split for a 2-person production team:

| Role | ₹/video | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Research + script | ₹1,200–1,500 | Protect this line first, it's the real bottleneck |
| AI voice generation | ₹200–400 | Test vidIQ's built-in voiceover vs. ElevenLabs on video 1 |
| Archival visuals / stock | ₹400–600 | |
| Editing + motion graphics | ₹800–1,000 | High-contrast figure + strategic element (map/ship/weapon) beats plain portraits ~2.5x |
| Thumbnail | ₹200–300 | |

One partner owns research/script (irreplaceable), the other owns the production pipeline
(voice, edit, thumbnail). Use the Shorts-to-longform funnel: a cheap 50-second teaser first,
greenlight the full 15-min cut only if it lands.
