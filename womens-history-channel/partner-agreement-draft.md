## What each partner delivers, concretely

**Flavio — 1 video brief per day, 1-2 hours.** Using `video-brief-template.md`: a real
name/case (not a bracket placeholder), 2-3 comparable videos from `roadmap.md` with what made
them work, and a storyboard (hook, 4-6 beats, close) — not a script. Handed to Rishabh same day.

**Rishabh — turns each brief into a full script.** Target: script delivered within 24-48 hours
of receiving a brief, so the script queue never falls more than 2 days behind Flavio's brief
queue.

**Joy — hires and manages the production side.** Editor, thumbnail person, and picks/tests the
AI voice tool (vidIQ voiceover vs. ElevenLabs, per the roadmap's open decision). Owns getting a
finished script turned into a published video.

## Format the team is committing to (per the roadmap)

- **Style:** faceless, AI-voiced, dark/injustice-framed history — not plain admiring biography.
  The roadmap's two failed channels (History Passion, GrimoryX) both used plain titles; that is
  the one thing to actively avoid.
- **Titles:** Template A (biography) or Template B (listicle erasure) only, for now. Template C
  (famous-name/counterfactual) is untested — flagged as a diagnostic experiment, not a default.
- **Budget:** ₹3,000/video, ₹24,000/month at 8 videos/month, split per the production table in
  `roadmap.md`.
- **Stop-loss:** ₹2 lakh total commitment, with review gates at Video 10, Video 20, and Video 40,
  and a 20K-views override (a video that clears 20K resets the clock on the gate it's sitting at).
  Anchor expectations to trailing 30-day view averages, not lifetime channel averages — see
  `roadmap.md` for why.
