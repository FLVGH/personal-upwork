## What was fixed (grammar / errors only)

- "shar e" → "share"
- Lowercase "i", "im", "ill", "dont" → capitalised, apostrophes added. Casual reads as human, missing apostrophes read as careless.
- Run-on: "i got a good hang of what im doing for the past few years I have been only consulting" had no sentence break.
- "youtube strategist" → "YouTube strategist"
- "Mr.Ballen" → "MrBallen" (his actual channel name)
- "sports niche" → "sports niches"
- "or easier share your channel" was broken. Now "Or easier, share your channel and I'll work on that."
- Ampersands → "and". Four of them read like private notes, not a proposal.
- Redundancy: "Not one lucky spike" and "repeat success not luck" say the same thing two lines apart. Kept the first, cut the second.
- Duplicate: "I'm confident I can help you achieve the success you want" at the top and "I can help you" at the bottom. Kept the top one, trimmed "achieve the success you want" (vague, no number behind it), cut the bottom one so the CTA lands clean.
- Moved the trial offer up. It was split across two paragraphs and "dont pay me $5 trial fee" repeated the opener.
